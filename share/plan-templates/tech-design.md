### Background

What does a reader need to know to understand the solutions proposed? Current
architecture, constraints, relevant user flows. A high-level design paragraph
and an overall system diagram (mermaid) usually pay for themselves. Skip
anything the brief sections above already cover.

### Scope

What this design must accomplish, and what is explicitly out of scope.

### Key decisions

The most important section. Most projects have a few decisions that are hard
to change and need alignment. One subsection per decision; under it, the
recommendation plus one Rejected entry for every alternative seriously
considered — with the trade-offs that decided it. Repeat the pattern; delete
what does not apply.

#### Decision: <name>

**Recommendation: <solution A>** — why it wins over the alternatives.

**Rejected: <solution B>** — why not, and what it would have bought.

**Rejected: <solution C>** — one entry per alternative considered.
